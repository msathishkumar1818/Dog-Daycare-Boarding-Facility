New Guest Protocol

The Free 2-Hour Evaluation Day

Every dog must complete our free evaluation before their first stay to ensure safe pack chemistry.

1

Health & Vaccine Review

Our registered veterinary technician checks proof of Rabies, DHPP, Bordetella, and performs a quick nose-to-tail wellness exam.

2

One-on Acclimation

Your dog spends 20 minutes exploring our suites and yards with a certified behavior specialist to establish trust and familiarity.

3

Mini-Pack Introduction

We introduce two calm, temperament-matched playmates one at a time, observing body language before clearing for group play.
